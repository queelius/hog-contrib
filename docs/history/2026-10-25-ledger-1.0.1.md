Alexander Richard Towell. Archive freeze: 2026-10-25 UTC.
Reserved version DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.23633619`; existing concept DOI:

## Scope

This release archives the completed 2026-10-21 prior-art assessment and the
2026-10-22 corrections to eight House of Graphs annotations. The 26 witnesses
are unchanged, as are their HoG IDs, verification parameters and per-record
evidence DOIs. Verifier code and tests are unchanged from `v1.0.0`.

The registry now records 17 known values, six new values, two new
classifications and one correction, with no unassessed records. These are
scoped ledger descriptions, not a new global novelty assessment. The order-8
C6 value is credited as known; uniqueness remains the classification claim.
The C5 attribution no longer applies Chen's large-order theorem to order 8.
The P4/P5 records credit the checked sources and distinguish the omitted P4
minimum family from the already known value. Full statements, locators and
limitations remain in each record's structured `claim`.

The annotation texts and live readbacks are preserved in
`receipts/2026-10-22-prior-art-annotations.json` and its attachment. This
release performs no new HoG action, census or research. C6 stays paused.

  Branch coverage was not measured. The adjacent

  statements; no new verifier behavior or improved coverage is claimed.
- Every witness file, graph6 mirror, count, HoG ID, verification block and
  backing DOI matches `v1.0.0`. The aggregate witness inventory SHA-256 is
  `1335e017d6ba598c8e26a3cf4a2473a157275eaeb4f3a2d44bc144efc16765d9`.
